What you're seeing
The brain's motor cortex has 3,486 neurons dedicated to speech (80-83% sub-range). Each neuron maps to a position in a 256-token vocabulary. When enough speech neurons fire, the peak neuron's position is decoded as a character.

Output is currently noise — the brain is still learning language patterns through sensory experience. This is equivalent to a baby's babbling stage. With continued audio+text training, meaningful tokens will emerge.
Phase: Adolescent — Pre-linguistic babble
